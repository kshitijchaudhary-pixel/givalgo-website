HOW IT WORKS

How Givalgo works for grant management and giving platforms.

1

Verify at onboarding and payout

Gate nonprofit sign-ups, matching, and disbursements on a live eligibility and sanctions check, including every officer and director.

2

Enrich your UI with organization data

Search and profiles from the Data API power lookups, autocomplete, and grantee pages. Data Pro adds 450+ fields per organization when you need depth.

3

Generate diligence briefs inside the grant lifecycle

The Research API returns a citation-backed brief for any organization, so reviewers never leave your product.

4

Stay current without a data team

Nightly refresh from IRS and sanctions sources, webhooks for status changes, and bulk endpoints for backfills.
